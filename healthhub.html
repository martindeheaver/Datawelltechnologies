HealthHub

Android application for gathering and managing personal health data

HealthHub is an Android application designed to gather users’ health data, supporting personal health tracking and insights while prioritising user control and privacy.

Purpose

The app enables individuals to collect and manage health-related information on their own device, forming a foundation for better personal health awareness and potential integration with other privacy-conscious health and AI systems developed by Datawell Technologies.

Focus areas

  • Health data collection on Android devices
  • User-controlled data ownership and privacy
  • Personal health tracking and insights

Key details

  • Status: In development
  • Platform: Android
  • Focus: Health data gathering, privacy, personal tracking

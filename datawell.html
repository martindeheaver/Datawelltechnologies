Datawell

Core platform and foundational data infrastructure

Datawell is the core platform and foundational data infrastructure of Datawell Technologies. It underpins our AI, health-data and IoT solutions, providing the shared layer for data handling, privacy controls and intelligent services across our portfolio.

Role in the portfolio

As the flagship platform, Datawell supports projects such as CADRIA, HealthHub, PAIX and CAIR by offering consistent approaches to data governance, local and secure processing, and integration with specialised AI systems.

Focus

  • Foundational data infrastructure
  • Privacy-first design principles
  • Support for AI, health and IoT applications
  • Enabling commercialisation of research-driven technologies

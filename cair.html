CAIR

Citizen-Centric AI for Resilient IoT Systems

We are delighted to have been selected as the commercialisation partner for the University of Southampton for CAIR — Citizen-Centric AI for Resilient IoT Systems.

CAIR is a privacy-first smart home AI assistant developed by researchers at the University of Southampton. Funded by an EPSRC Impact Acceleration Account (IAA), the system is designed to run entirely on a user’s own local device without relying on external cloud data centres. It aims to understand everyday language and naturally adapt to user preferences over time while prioritising data privacy.

Core philosophy

Privacy-by-design. Private home voice and sensor data do not need to be sent to the cloud. Processing stays on the user’s device, giving people greater control over their personal information.

Technology focus

  • Smart home automation
  • Internet of Things (IoT)
  • Localised / on-device machine learning
  • Natural language understanding and personal adaptation

Funding & support

  • Backed by the EPSRC Impact Acceleration Account (IAA)
  • In late 2026, selected for Innovate UK’s ICURe Discover programme for market validation and progression toward commercialisation

Key details

  • Full name: Citizen-Centric AI for Resilient IoT Systems
  • Partner: University of Southampton
  • Role: Commercialisation partner (Datawell Technologies)
  • Funding: EPSRC IAA + Innovate UK ICURe Discover
  • Status: Commercialisation / market validation
  • Key principle: Privacy-by-design, fully local processing

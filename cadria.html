CADRIA.org

Consensus-Anchored Digital Representative Infrastructure for Agents

CADRIA is a system designed to improve the reliability and accountability of AI outputs. Rather than relying solely on undifferentiated AI ensembles or ad-hoc human consultation, CADRIA dynamically assembles a task-specific network of specialised evaluators — Human Digital Representatives (HDRs) — to generate a consensus score on the quality, safety and actionability of an AI response.

How it works

Human Digital Representatives are specialised AI systems that are:

  • Trained under the supervision of identifiable human experts or professional bodies
  • Owned or stewarded by those experts or organisations
  • Validated against domain-specific performance benchmarks
  • Continuously audited and aligned with professional standards

CADRIA selects an optimal sub-network of HDRs based on domain relevance, task criticality, past performance, user feedback and cost-efficiency. The resulting consensus score is a structured aggregation of domain-qualified digital expertise, weighted by competence, relevance and risk sensitivity.

Why it matters

This architecture enables scalable, near-real-time validation of AI outputs while preserving accountability and professional standards. It also creates a digitally mediated marketplace for expertise, allowing human experts and professional organisations to extend their reach beyond traditional consulting models.
